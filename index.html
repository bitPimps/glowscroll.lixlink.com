Your phone is the sign.

What it does

A big, bright sign in seconds

Everything you need to hold up a sign, and nothing you don’t.

Your sign in one tap

Type, tap Show, hold it up. Quick phrases like “THANK YOU!” go full screen in a single tap, and Show last puts your last sign back up.

Looks like real LEDs

Round LED dots with a soft glow, or smooth text. Any color, including your own, plus rainbow and blinking. Fit-to-height text is readable from across a room.

Any language, any emoji

Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese, Hindi and full emoji are drawn correctly, not as boxes.

Built for crowds

Full brightness and keep-awake while showing. Tap to pause; hold to exit, so a bump in the crowd won’t close your sign.

Mirror mode

Flip the sign to read correctly through a window or glass. Only use it while parked.

Flash Safety

On by default: blinking stays at three flashes a second or fewer, below the level known to trigger photosensitive seizures.

Privacy by design

Your signs stay on your phone.

GlowScroll has no account system and no server holding your signs. The app has no ads and no analytics, so nobody is watching what you write.

  • No sign-up, no email address, no account.
  • Your banners are stored on your device.
  • No ads and no tracking in the app.
  • Optional backup goes to your own iCloud or Google Drive.
  • Works without an internet connection.
