Delete your GlowScroll data

Last updated October 13, 2026

GlowScroll has no account and no server, so there’s no copy of your signs anywhere but your device. Here’s how to remove them.

Delete one sign

On the home screen, tap Saved, then Delete next to the sign.

Delete everything

Uninstall GlowScroll. That removes every sign and setting from the device. On iPhone, an older iCloud or computer backup of your phone may still include GlowScroll’s data until that backup is replaced or deleted (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups).

Purchase records

If you bought Premium, Apple or Google keeps the record of your purchase under your store account, and RevenueCat holds an anonymous record so the purchase can be restored. To ask for that record to be deleted, email glowscroll-support@lixlink.com. Deleting it means Restore purchase can no longer find it; your purchase history with Apple or Google isn’t affected.
