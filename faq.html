Getting started

What is GlowScroll?

GlowScroll turns your phone or tablet into a bright scrolling LED sign. Type a message, tap Show, and hold your phone up. It has no ads, no account and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up and no login. Open the app and type your sign.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Showing, editing and saving signs all work without an internet connection.

Showing a sign

How do I show a sign full screen?

Type your message on the home screen and tap Show (or the Go key on your keyboard). Quick phrases go full screen with one tap, and Show last puts your last sign back up.

How do I pause or exit?

Tap the sign to pause or resume it. To exit, press and hold for about a second and a half, so a bump in a crowd doesn’t close your sign. You can also use the back gesture or back button.

Why does my screen go to full brightness?

A sign is easiest to read at full brightness, so GlowScroll turns it up while the sign is showing and puts it back when you exit.

How do I change the look, color or speed?

Tap Style on the home screen. You can pick the LED look, dot size and glow, text and background colors (tap Custom for any color), direction (left, right or still), speed, and effects like blink and rainbow. The preview at the top shows every change.

What is mirror mode?

Mirror flips the sign so it reads correctly from the other side of a window or glass. Only use a sign in a car while parked, and never where it blocks your view.

What is Flash Safety?

Flash Safety is on by default. It keeps blinking to three flashes a second or fewer, below the level known to trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. You can turn it off in Settings, but only if nobody nearby could be affected.

How do I use a template?

Tap Templates on the home screen, pick a category and tap a sign. It opens in Style, where you can change the words, colors or look before showing it.

Which fonts are there?

Four fonts are free: Bold, Silkscreen, Arcade and Terminal. Premium adds eight more. Pick one under Font in Style.

Saved banners

Can I organize saved signs into folders?

Yes, with Premium. In Saved, tap “+ Folder”, then use “Move” on a sign to put it in a folder. Deleting a folder keeps its signs saved.

How do I save a sign?

Open Style, set up your sign and tap Save. Saved signs are listed under Saved on the home screen, where you can show, edit, copy or delete them.

How many signs can I save?

The free version keeps 20 saved signs. Premium removes the limit. Your recent signs are always listed on the home screen as well.

Privacy & your data

Where are my signs stored?

On your device. GlowScroll has no server and no account system, so we never receive what you write.

Does the app track me?

No. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. This website uses Google Analytics for basic visit counts; the app doesn’t. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete your GlowScroll data. In short: delete a sign from Saved, use Settings → Erase all banners, or uninstall the app.

Backup & restore

How do I move my signs to a new phone?

In Settings → Backup & export, tap Export banners to save your saved signs and folders as one file. Save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Files, Google Drive, email). On the new phone, install GlowScroll and tap Import a backup file.

What does automatic backup do?

With Premium, GlowScroll saves a copy of your saved signs and folders to a private folder in your own Google Drive a short while after you change something. Only GlowScroll can open that folder, and the copy never goes to our servers.

Why does Android ask me to sign in with Google?

Google Drive needs your permission before GlowScroll can save there. GlowScroll asks only for its own hidden app folder, not your other Drive files.

How do I restore from my backup?

Install GlowScroll, unlock or restore Premium, then go to Settings → Backup & export and tap Restore from backup. On Android, link Google Drive first. Restoring adds your backed-up signs to the phone. It only removes a sign if you deleted it on another phone.

I set up a new phone. Will its first backup erase my old one?

No. The first backup on a phone brings in what’s already in your backup before it saves, so nothing in it is lost.

I use GlowScroll on two phones. Do they stay in step?

Mostly, when both have backup on with the same Google account. Each phone brings in the other’s saved signs when it next backs up, and a sign deleted on one phone is deleted on the other too. Your recent (unsaved) signs stay on each phone.

Premium & purchases

What does Premium include?

The Square LED, Pixel and Neon looks; Sparkle, Wave, Typewriter and Color cycle effects; gradient text; exact dot size and glow; eight more fonts; unlimited saved signs and folders; automatic backup to your own Google Drive; and every future Premium feature. It’s a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Can I try Premium looks first?

Yes. Pick any Premium look or effect in Style to preview it. Shown full screen, it stays for 30 seconds before switching to a free look. To save a sign that uses one, unlock Premium, or tap Use free look to save it with free settings instead.

I bought Premium. How do I get it on a new phone?

Install GlowScroll, signed in to the same Google account, then go to Settings → Restore purchase.

Is Family Sharing supported?

No. Google Play doesn’t let family members share one-time purchases, so Premium belongs to the Google account that bought it.

Troubleshooting

My sign looks choppy.

Close other apps and try again. If it’s still choppy, email us your phone model so we can look into it.
