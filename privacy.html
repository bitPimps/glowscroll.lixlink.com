Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

GlowScroll (“the app”) is made by THE LIX GROUP LLC. It’s built so there’s very little to say in a privacy policy: we don’t run servers for the app, there’s no account system, and we don’t collect what you write. This page explains what that means. It also covers the basic analytics on this website.

What GlowScroll stores, and where

GlowScroll stores the signs you show and save, and your settings, in a database on your device. We have no server that receives, processes or stores this information, and no way to access it.

GlowScroll opts out of Android’s automatic device backup. On iPhone, your phone’s own iCloud or computer backup includes GlowScroll’s data, as it does for most apps, under Apple’s terms.

Accounts and identity

GlowScroll doesn’t ask for your name, email address or any other identifying information. There’s no login and no account system.

Permissions

GlowScroll doesn’t ask for any permissions to show a sign. It raises your screen’s brightness and keeps the screen on only while a sign is showing, which needs no permission. It never asks for your location, contacts, microphone or camera.

Purchases

You buy Premium through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, and Apple or Google processes the purchase under its own privacy policy. We use RevenueCat to validate your purchase; RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous app identifier to do this, under its own privacy policy. GlowScroll only learns whether you’ve unlocked Premium, never your name or payment details.

Analytics and advertising

The GlowScroll app doesn’t include third-party analytics or advertising tools, and it doesn’t track how you use it. Nothing in the app is sent to Google Analytics.

This website works differently. It uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), loaded through Google Tag Manager, for basic traffic measurement: which pages get visited, roughly where visitors are, what device and browser they use, and how they found the site. Google may set cookies to do this. We don’t use the data to identify you, and we don’t run ads on the site. Google’s handling of the data falls under Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Deleting your data

Everything GlowScroll stores is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. See Delete your GlowScroll data for details.

Children’s privacy

GlowScroll isn’t directed at children. The app collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature changes how data is handled, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Send questions about this policy or how GlowScroll handles data to glowscroll-support@lixlink.com.
